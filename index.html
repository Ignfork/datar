// HOW THE ROOM WORKS

four steps from briefing to payout

01_BRIEF.TXT

Brief

Name, ticker, image and socials. Pick what to pair against: SOL, a stablecoin, a tokenized stock or another token.
02_TAX.CFG

Set the tax

Choose a trading tax from 0 to 5%, then route it to the creator, team, marketing tank or straight to holders.
03_LAUNCH.BAT

Launch

The coin goes live on a Raydium LaunchLab curve, then graduates into a Meteora pool and shows up on the radar.
04_PAYOUT.EXE

Get paid

Every 10 minutes, holders above the minimum get their share. The marketing tank buys boosts on its own.
